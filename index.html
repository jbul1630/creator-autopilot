What Creator Autopilot does

The application provides a workflow for creating original short-form content while keeping the creator in control of review and publishing.

Content ideas

Generate and organize short-form content ideas for the creator's workflow.

Script generation

Turn selected ideas into structured scripts ready for review and production.

Video creation

Render short-form videos from approved scripts and production assets.

Creator-controlled publishing

Connect an account securely and transfer content only after explicit user authorization.

TikTok integration

Creator Autopilot uses TikTok's official authorization and content publishing interfaces. The creator remains responsible for the final content and publishing decision.

Connect your TikTok account

The user is redirected to TikTok to authenticate and explicitly authorize the permissions requested by Creator Autopilot.

Review the content

The creator reviews the generated video before any transfer or publishing action takes place.

Transfer or publish

Depending on the selected workflow and available permissions, the video can be uploaded to the creator's TikTok inbox or sent for publishing after explicit confirmation.

Stay in control

Creator Autopilot does not authorize access to a TikTok account without the user's consent and does not sell TikTok user data.

Privacy and security

Creator Autopilot is designed around explicit authorization and limited use of account data required for the integration.

Data and account control

TikTok authorization data is used only to provide the requested integration. Access credentials are stored securely by the application and are not intentionally exposed to other users. Users can stop using the service and revoke access through the available account controls.
